# **ABOUT HOTEL PRABHUPADA**

Hotel Prabhupada is a hospitality property located in Puri, Odisha, offering comfortable accommodation and guest services for travellers visiting the city.

The hotel provides different room categories suitable for couples, families, groups, and individual travellers. Guests can choose from standard accommodation options as well as sea-view room categories.

Hotel Prabhupada offers facilities such as Wi-Fi, room service, housekeeping, parking, swimming pool access, restaurant services, travel assistance, and other guest conveniences.

The hotel is located near the sea beach and provides convenient access to popular destinations in and around Puri, including Shree Jagannath Temple, Puri Sea Beach, Swargadwar, Konark, and Chilika Lake.

Hotel Prabhupada aims to provide guests with a comfortable and convenient stay while visiting Puri.
